I'm Sebas

BASHI. Director, editor and photographer. I turn ideas into images with a cinematic texture.

  • Based inBogotá, Colombia
  • FromFusagasugá, Colombia
  • I doDirecting, editing, photography, vocal mixing, beats and websites
  • CitiesNew York, Miami, Atlanta, Santo Domingo, Bogotá

It all started out of curiosity: as a kid I was handed a professional camera and never let it go. By 15 I had my own and had begun creating; at 17 I earned a technical degree in film that gave me my foundation. Today, at 23, I've been editing video professionally for over six years: I started self-taught and kept refining until I made it my full-time craft.

My work feels different because I'm dynamic, adaptive and contemporary: I shape each project around what the client needs, and creatively I explore many cinematic directions. I love playing between analog and digital, pulling digital footage into an analog texture without losing quality. What I enjoy most is the edit: editing is where I truly tell the story.

I'm also a vocal recording and mixing engineer and I make beats, so I often score or mix the audio of the same video, so sound and image speak the same language. On the music side I'm part of Estación 2600, a recording studio and creative hub in Bogotá.

I've worked with artists like Kanye West, French Montana, Offset and Javiielo, plus artists and businesses across New York, Miami, Atlanta, the Dominican Republic and Colombia. I'm from Fusagasugá: a small-town guy who learned to move through the big city without losing his roots. My philosophy is simple: never stop learning.
