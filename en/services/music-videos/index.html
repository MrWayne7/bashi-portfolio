Music videos

I direct, shoot and edit music videos. I've made videos for North West, Asian Doll, Santa Mykah, Essene and Adonys X, among others. Since I also record vocals and make beats, I understand the song before I think about the picture.

Artists, labels and producers who want a video with identity, not a template.

Concept

We start from the song and land on a clear visual idea, with references.

Shoot

FAQ

How long does a music video take?

It depends on the concept. A one-day shoot is usually delivered in one to two weeks, with one round of changes.

Can you edit footage shot by another crew?

Yes. I also edit music videos shot by other teams, remotely.

Do you work with US artists?

Yes. Editing is remote, and I can travel for shoots.
